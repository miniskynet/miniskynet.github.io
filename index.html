Overview

With a BEng (Hons) in Software Engineering from the University of Westminster and hands-on experience building real-world applications, I bring genuine technical depth to the work of documentation and developer experience. Before moving into technical writing I spent time as a software engineer across full-stack web, Android, and API layers — Java, JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, SQL, Spring Boot, Bash scripts, Ansible playbooks, Linux environments, and open source contributions. That background means I can read a codebase, understand what developers are building, and document it without needing everything explained from scratch.

I also write about software engineering on Medium — one explainer on Python operators was curated and featured by Medium's editorial team, reaching 2.5K+ readers. If you're building a developer-facing product and need someone who genuinely understands both sides, feel free to reach out.
